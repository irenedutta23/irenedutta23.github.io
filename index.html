01 — About

Physics, data and hardware, end to end.

I'm a Research Associate at Fermi National Accelerator Laboratory working on the Compact Muon Solenoid (CMS) experiment at the Large Hadron Collider. My work spans the full chain of a modern physics measurement: designing and testing the detectors that collect the data, building the machine-learning tools that separate rare signals from overwhelming backgrounds, and leading the statistical analyses that turn them into published results.

I've coordinated international analysis teams, led the global CMS combination of di-Higgs searches, chaired Fermilab's LPC Physics Forum, and mentored more than a dozen graduate students and many undergraduates. I'm now looking for my next role, where I can bring that mix of quantitative depth, technical leadership and hands-on engineering.

02 — Research

Research highlights

Searches for rare Higgs processes and the detector technology needed to see them at the High-Luminosity LHC and beyond.

Physics · Lead post-doc2023 – now

HH → bbγγ with Run 3 CMS data

  • Probes the Higgs self-coupling through the clean diphoton signature.
  • Pioneered a new boosted category using ParticleNet.
  • RNN and BDT classifiers reaching 85% background rejection at 95% signal efficiency.
Delivered the first CMS HH result on 2022–23 data (CMS-PAS-HIG-25-007).
Physics · Coordinator2026 – now

HH → bbττ with scouting Run 3 data

  • Coordinating the first CMS effort to use Run 3 scouting datasets for resonant and non-resonant HH.
  • Built the first ML-based b-jet and τ taggers (particle transformers) for reduced scouting reconstruction.
Public results targeted for summer 2027.
Physics · Coordinator2025 – now

H → μμ with Run 2 + Run 3 data

  • High-priority search with potential for a 5σ observation of second-generation Yukawa couplings.
  • Leading the most sensitive ggF and VBF categories with BDT/DNN classifiers and robust background modelling.
Builds on my Ph.D. work giving the first evidence (3.0σ) for H → μμ.
Physics · Co-lead2022 – 2026

Combination of Run 2 di-Higgs searches

  • Combined 11 separate CMS Run 2 HH searches.
  • Added EFT and UV-complete BSM model re-interpretations.
Most comprehensive CMS result on the Higgs self-coupling to date (J. Phys. G, 2026).
Physics · Ph.D.2020 – 2022

HH → bbbb with boosted large-radius jets

  • Led the first ever dedicated boosted HH search; lead analyst for the ggF category.
  • Derived data-driven corrections, studied systematics and ran the statistical analysis.
Best sensitivity to the SM HH cross section among CMS searches; first exclusion of κ2V = 0 (PRL 2023).
Detector · Lead2022 – now

Endcap Timing Layer (ETL) upgrade

  • Leading QA/QC design for production of 2,000 ETL modules at Fermilab, to be adopted by sites worldwide.
  • Built a full-system test stand (laser, beta source, cosmics) and iterated the PCB readout board.
  • Led test-beam campaigns at FNAL, DESY and CERN.
Demonstrated ~45 ps module timing across varied conditions.
Detector · Lead2022 – now

AC-LGAD sensors for future 4D trackers

  • Characterized prototype sensors at multiple Fermilab beam tests across varied geometries.
20 ps time and 10 µm spatial resolution; set the baseline sensor choice for the ePIC detector at the EIC.
Detector · Lead tester2022 – now

Constant-fraction discriminator ASIC

  • Tested custom CFD chips for AC-LGAD timing readout in the ePIC barrel TOF layer, removing the need for offline calibration.
Achieved 35 ps with both DC- and AC-LGADs.
Detector · Ph.D.2017 – 2022

Barrel Timing Layer (BTL) upgrade

  • Tested SiPM + LYSO modules with TOFPET2 and TOFHIR2 readout at Caltech, CERN and FNAL.
  • Led crystal-geometry optimization and thermal-control studies of irradiated SiPMs.
Reached the target ≤30 ps resolution at test beams.
Computing · Ph.D.2019 – 2020

HEP analysis with GPU-accelerated kernels

  • Built a fast, kernel-based analysis library with Python + Numba on multi-threaded CPUs and CUDA on GPUs (hepaccelerate, hepaccelerate-cms).
A full end-to-end analysis of the entire Run 2 dataset in ~2 hours.
Detector · Ph.D.2018 – 2019

CMS HCAL calibration with muon MIPs

  • Developed a new calibration technique for the SiPM readout of the HCAL Phase-I endcap using muons, applied to 2018 collision data.
